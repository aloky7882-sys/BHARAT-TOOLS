Cookies Policy

This is the Cookies Policy for ⚡ BHARAT-TOOLS 🚀. We use cookies to enhance and smooth out your experience while interacting with our free web utilities.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files downloaded to your laptop, desktop, or mobile device browser to improve website mechanics. This page describes what information they gather and why we sometimes need to store these preferences.

How We Use Cookies

We use essential cookies to remember adjustments you make inside our web applications (like saving recent math calculation strings in the Scientific Calculator History tracker). This data stays local to your terminal environment.

Third-Party Cookies (AdSense & Analytics)

In the near future, we may deploy third-party advertising modules like Google AdSense. These networks automatically deploy DART tracking cookies to read standard metadata variables and serve targeted, relevant ads based on browsing tendencies across the web.

Disabling Cookies

You can prevent the deployment of cookies by adjusting the operational configurations inside your device browser (check your browser's Help menu to learn how). Note that disabling cookie management might degrade the performance trackers of your saved configuration histories.
